### **Our Three Core Architectural Pillars**

1. **Deterministic Mathematics Over Black-Box AI**  
   We calculate facts—we never hallucinate them. All performance scores, KPI percentages, sales commissions, and attendance determinations use pure mathematical formulas. AI is strictly barred from making or modifying promotion, compensation, disciplinary, or termination decisions.

2. **Respectful Accountability Over Invasive Surveillance**  
   No background keyloggers. No secret desktop screen grabbers. No 24/7 mobile GPS stalking. Accountability is measured where it truly matters: physical presence at shift boundaries and verifiable delivery of peer-reviewed work.

3. **Built-In Data Privacy & Local Zero-Retention Voice**  
   Transcriptions run locally on host servers using `faster-whisper`. Voice audio is processed in memory and shredded from disk in strict code `finally` blocks. Your sensitive company data is never sent to third-party cloud speech providers.

# [PANEL 3: MODULAR POWERHOUSES — CAPABILITIES OVERVIEW]

### 1. 📍 Smart Geofenced Attendance & Leave Management
* **Zero-Hardware Browser Check-In**: Employees tap "Check In" on their mobile or laptop browser.
* **Haversine 300m Spherical Geofence**: Validates physical presence within 300 meters of registered company facilities.
* **GPS Anti-Spoofing Guard**: Rejects location readings with accuracy $>200\text{m}$ to block emulator spoofing.
* **Predictable Rules**: Automatic `LATE` classification past 11:30 AM IST; automatic `HALF_DAY` demotion for shifts under 240 minutes.
* **Integrated Leave Quotas**: Multi-tier leave quotas (Privilege, Casual, Sick) with live manager approval workflows.

### 2. 📋 Visual Task Kanban & Delivery Velocity
* **7-Stage Interactive State Machine**: `Not Started` $\to$ `In Progress` $\to$ `Blocked` $\to$ `In Review` $\to$ `Completed` $\to$ `Reopened`.
* **Instant Blocker Escalation**: Flag technical or client impediments; immediately surfaces alerts on managerial cockpits.
* **Cycle Time & Rework Tracking**: Automatically measures ticket velocity and rework loops to identify requirement gaps.
* **Append-Only Activity Stream**: Immutable historical audit trail for every task reassignment, priority shift, and discussion.

### 3. 🎙️ Local Voice-to-Task AI (11 Languages + English)
* **Multilingual Speech Recognition**: Supports English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, and Urdu.
* **Intent-Aware NLP Parser**: Distinguishes between prospective tasks ("need to complete X tomorrow") and retrospective updates ("finished client slides in 2 hours").
* **Spoken Duration Extraction**: Automatically converts spoken numbers ("two hours", "दो घंटे") into numeric task durations.
* **Human-in-the-Loop Confirmation**: Displays an editable preview card before committing any voice command to the database.

### 4. 🎯 Objective Performance & Cryptographic Snapshots
* **Cascading Strategic Goals**: Align corporate, departmental, and individual objectives with percentage weightings.
* **Measurable KPIs with Bounded Achievement**: Real-time performance tracking bounded between $0\%$ and $200\%$.
* **Transparent Formula Scoring**: Balanced evaluation across Goals ($W_g$), KPIs ($W_k$), and 360-degree Competencies ($W_r$).
* **Frozen Audit Snapshots**: Historical evaluations are frozen into tamper-evident records that cannot be retroactively altered.

### 5. 🗺️ Commercial Sales Intelligence & 7-Tier GIS
* **7-Tier Geographic Hierarchy**: Track market penetration from `Global` $\to$ `Country` $\to$ `State` $\to$ `District` $\to$ `City` $\to$ `Area` $\to$ `Pincode`.
* **Territory Capacity Math**: Computes required rep headcount and flags overloaded territories before leads are neglected.
* **Lead SLA & Lost Revenue Warning**: Calculates estimated lost revenue when sales prospects sit unattended past response SLAs.
* **Idempotent Realized Revenue Ledger**: Closed-won deals automatically book verified revenue to the financial ledger with zero double-counting.
* **5-Tier Compensation Engine**: Configure Proportional, Commission Slabs, Flat, Target-Gate, or Hybrid incentive payouts.

### 6. 🏆 Capability Matrix, Skill Heatmaps & Timed Assessments

* **Verified vs. Self-Reported Evidence**: Validate skill claims against pull requests, certificates, and portfolio links.
