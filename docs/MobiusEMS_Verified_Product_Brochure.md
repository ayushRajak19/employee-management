# 1. Executive Overview & Core Purpose

### The Operational Challenge: Escaping the "5-Tool Trap"
Most growing companies struggle with fragmented software tools that do not talk to each other:
* **Hardware Time Clocks**: Break down often, create morning entrance queues, and are useless for remote or field staff.
* **Isolated Task Boards**: Great for logging tickets, but completely disconnected from employee attendance, capacity, and reviews.
* **Spreadsheet-Driven Appraisals**: Annual reviews conducted months after work was done, suffering from recency bias and personal favoritism.
* **Standalone Sales CRMs**: Expensive sales tools disconnected from employee workload, leave calendars, and verified accounting ledgers.
* **Scattered Records**: Resumes in inboxes, training records in spreadsheets, and zero centralized compliance audit trails.

### The MobiusEMS Solution
**MobiusEMS** unifies daily work execution, verified geofenced attendance, talent capabilities, objective performance evidence, and commercial sales territories into one coherent, role-aware operating workspace.

### Our Three Guiding Principles
1. **Deterministic Mathematics Over Guesses**: All performance scores, KPI percentages, attendance rules, and sales metrics use transparent mathematical calculations. AI is strictly an advisory layer and is barred from making employment, salary, promotion, or termination decisions.
2. **Respectful Accountability Over Spyware**: No background keyloggers, no secret screen grabbers, and no 24/7 mobile GPS stalking. Accountability is measured where it matters: verified physical presence at shift boundaries and verifiable completion of peer-reviewed work.
3. **Local Zero-Retention Voice Privacy**: Speech-to-task recognition runs locally on your host server. Spoken commands are transcribed and the ephemeral audio is shredded immediately. Voice recordings are never sent to third-party cloud speech APIs and never stored on disk.

# 3. Smart Geofenced Attendance & Leave Management

### Zero-Hardware Browser-Based Check-In
* **No Biometric Hardware Required**: Employees check in directly on their mobile or laptop browser.
* **300-Meter Office Geofence**: Validates that check-in occurs within 300 meters of the registered office location using spherical distance calculations.
* **GPS Anti-Spoofing Protection**: Automatically rejects location readings with accuracy fuzzier than 200 meters to prevent fake location apps and mock GPS emulators.

### Automated Shift Determinations
* **11:30 AM Late Cutoff**: Check-ins completed after 11:30 AM IST are automatically marked as Late.
* **4-Hour Minimum Shift Rule**: Upon checkout, any shift totaling under 240 minutes (4 hours) of elapsed time is automatically demoted to a Half-Day.
* **Clear Daily Statuses**: Categorizes every workday into Present, Late, Half-Day, On Leave, or Absent.

### Attendance Regularization & Manager Sign-Off
* **Field Travel & Issue Resolution**: When client visits or transit delays prevent standard check-in, employees submit a regularization claim with supporting notes.
* **Direct Manager Approval**: The request routes to the reporting manager; approval updates daily attendance and records a permanent audit entry.

### Leave Quota Tracking
* **Custom Leave Balances**: Manage annual quotas for Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Paid Leave, Unpaid Leave, and Work From Home.
* **Date Overlap Guard**: Blocks overlapping or duplicate leave submissions automatically.
* **Live Department Calendar**: Approved leaves immediately reflect on team availability calendars.

### Real-Time Attendance Register
* **Executive & HR Visibility**: Real-time morning register showing attendance status across all departments, late arrivals, approved leaves, and unexpected absences.

# 4. Project Delivery, Kanban & Work Execution

### Interactive 7-Stage Visual Kanban
* **Structured State Transitions**: Tasks move through strictly validated stages:
  **Not Started → In Progress → In Review → Completed**
  *(with supported exception paths for Blocked, Reopened, or Cancelled)*.
* **Prevents Workflow Skips**: Direct jumps from Not Started to Completed are blocked, ensuring review integrity.

### Real-Time Blocker Escalation
* **Flag Impediments Instantly**: Team members can flag tasks as Blocked and specify the exact roadblock:
  - External Dependency (e.g., waiting for client credentials or third-party APIs)
  - Missing Design Assets (e.g., UI/UX mocks missing)
  - Infrastructure Outage (e.g., staging server down)
  - Requirement Ambiguity (e.g., unclear business logic)
* **Manager Alerts**: Blocked tickets immediately alert managers and department heads to resolve bottlenecks.
* **Fairness Protection**: External dependencies are flagged so employees are not penalized for delays outside their control.

### Cycle Time & Quality Rework Tracking
* **Velocity Metrics**: Measures elapsed hours from task start to completion.
* **Rework Counter**: Automatically increments whenever a task fails review and bounces back to In Progress or Reopened, pinpointing requirement ambiguities without finger-pointing.

### Centralized Task Tracker Grid
* **Multi-Project Grid**: Filter and sort all company tasks by assignee, priority (Low, Medium, High, Critical), project, status, and deadline.
* **Workload Visibility**: Quickly identify approaching deadlines and unbalanced task distribution.

### Append-Only Task Activity Stream
* **Audit Accountability**: Every priority update, status shift, reassignment, and discussion comment is permanently logged with actor name and timestamp.

# 5. Local Zero-Retention Voice-to-Task AI

### 11 Regional Indian Languages + English
* **Multilingual Voice Capture**: Field staff, sales reps, and engineers can speak in English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, or Urdu.

### Guaranteed Audio Shredding (Zero Cloud Exposure)
* **100% Private Local Transcription**: Audio is transcribed on your local host server. The ephemeral audio file is processed in memory and shredded immediately. Recordings are never sent to external cloud APIs and never stored on disk.

### Intelligent Intent & Duration Parsing
* **Prospective vs. Retrospective**:
  - Future tasks ("need to complete report by tomorrow") → Automatically creates a task in To-Do.
  - Completed work ("finished client presentation in 3 hours") → Moves task to In Review with 3 actual hours logged.
* **Spoken Number Conversion**: Converts spoken phrases ("two hours", "दो घंटे") into numeric durations.
* **Hinglish Compound Splitting**: Splits multi-part instructions ("Ayush ko frontend do aur Priya ko backend do") into distinct task assignments.

### Human-in-the-Loop Confirmation
* **Editable Preview**: Extracted task details (title, owner, hours, priority) are displayed on an editable preview card before saving to the database.

* **Strategic Alignment**: Connect company-wide goals down to departments and individuals with clear deadlines and weightings.
* **Quantifiable KPIs**: Measurable target vs. actual metrics with bounded achievement between 0% and 200%.

* **Multi-Competency Rubrics**: Standardized evaluation templates covering technical execution, teamwork, communication, and leadership.
* **Balanced Feedback**: Evaluates inputs from managers, peers, and employee self-reviews.

### Deterministic Mathematical Scoring Formula
* **Transparent Calculations**: Composite review scores are calculated mathematically:
  **Final Score = (Goals Achievement × Weight) + (KPI Performance × Weight) + (360 Reviews × Weight)**
* **Objective Performance Bands**:
  - 90%+ : **Exceptional**
  - 75% – 89.9% : **Strong Performer**
  - 60% – 74.9% : **Consistent Performer**
  - Below 60% : **Developing / Needs Support**

### Immutable Frozen Review Snapshots
* **Audit-Proof Records**: Once a review cycle closes, all scores, comments, and weights are frozen into an immutable snapshot record that cannot be altered retroactively.
