1. Why Growing Companies Need MobiusEMS

Running a growing business is hard when your everyday tools don't talk to each other. Most teams end up trapped in a daily juggling act:
* The Biometric Headache: Fingerprint machines fail, create long morning lines at the door, and are completely useless for field staff or remote days.
* Work Chaos on Chat: Important tasks get lost in WhatsApp chats and messy spreadsheets. Nobody knows who is working on what until a deadline is missed.
* Biased Year-End Appraisals: Annual performance reviews turn into popularity contests and memory games because managers don't have records of what was actually delivered.
* Disconnected Sales Teams: Sales reps log leads in expensive standalone CRMs that have no clue about employee availability, leave days, or verified accounting.
* Scattered Files & Resumes: Candidate resumes sit buried in HR inboxes, training records live in separate Excel files, and there is no single source of truth.

What is MobiusEMS?

MobiusEMS is an all-in-one workforce operating system built by Whalexy. It unifies your attendance, project execution, talent recruitment, skill development, objective performance appraisals, and commercial sales into one clean, role-based platform.

Built Around Three Core Values:

  1. Real Data, Zero Office Politics: Performance, KPI achievement, and attendance are tracked through transparent, objective calculations. AI is here to assist, never to make hiring, firing, or promotion decisions.
  2. Respectful Accountability, Not Surveillance: No secret screen recording, no creepy keystroke loggers, and no 24/7 GPS tracking. We verify physical presence when your shift starts, and track real deliverables that get finished.
  3. Total Voice Privacy: When you speak tasks into MobiusEMS, audio is transcribed on your local server and immediately deleted from memory. Nothing is ever sent to third-party cloud speech providers or saved on disk.

  • Living Career Timeline: A permanent chronological history of every promotion, title change, salary adjustment, department transfer, and award earned throughout the employee's journey.

Interactive Company Hierarchy & Visual Org Chart

  • Configurable Departments & Teams: Build custom departments, sub-teams, and designations that match how your business actually runs.
  • Visual Org Chart: Click through your company reporting hierarchy from senior executives down to individual team members. Easily spot open positions, view reporting lines, and balance team sizes.

Smooth Onboarding & First-Day Security

  • Quick HR Invites: HR creates a profile in minutes and sends the new hire an invitation link with temporary login details.
  • Mandatory Password Reset on First Login: Keeps company data safe by requiring new hires to set a private, secure password before accessing anything.
  • Resumable Onboarding Steps: New employees can fill in their background, upload ID proofs, and add emergency contacts at their own pace without losing entered data.

3. Smart Geofenced Attendance & Leave Tracking

Your Phone is Your Punch Card (No Hardware Required)

  • Zero Machine Costs: No biometric machines to buy, install, wire, or fix when they break. Employees check in using their phone or laptop browser.
  • Smart 300-Meter Office Geofence: The system checks the employee's location when they hit Check-In, confirming they are within 300 meters of the office.
  • Anti-Spoofing Filter: Automatically blocks fake GPS apps and mock location emulators by discarding low-accuracy signals (above 200m).

Automatic Shift Rules That Save HR Hours

  • 11:30 AM Late Cutoff: Anyone punching in after 11:30 AM is automatically marked Late, eliminating manual morning log checks.
  • 4-Hour Minimum Half-Day Rule: If an employee works less than 240 minutes (4 hours) between check-in and checkout, the day automatically logs as a Half-Day.
  • Clear Daily Statuses: Every day is neatly tagged as Present, Late, Half-Day, On Leave, or Absent.

Self-Service Regularization & Leave Management

  • Easy Regularization: Traveling for a client meeting or stuck in bad transit? Employees submit a quick note explaining the delay. Once their manager clicks approve, the daily record updates automatically.
  • Custom Leave Quotas: Manage yearly allowances for Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Paid Leave, Unpaid Leave, and Work-From-Home days.
  • Overlap Protection: Prevents duplicate or overlapping leave applications automatically.
  • Team Leave Calendar: Managers and team members can see upcoming approved leaves at a glance, making sprint planning straightforward.

4. Project Delivery, Kanban & Daily Work

Visual 7-Stage Kanban Boards

  • Clear Stages of Work: Tasks flow naturally through validated stages:
    Not Started → In Progress → In Review → Completed
    (with special handling for Blocked, Reopened, or Cancelled tasks).
  • Prevents Cheating the Workflow: Tasks cannot jump straight from Not Started to Completed without going through work and review stages.

Instant Roadblock Alerts (Blocker Escalation)

  • Flag What's Stuck Immediately: When a team member hits a wall, they flag the task as Blocked and select the exact reason:
  • Waiting on External Dependency (client feedback, vendor API, credentials)
  • Missing Assets (designs, copy, files)
  • Server / Infrastructure Outage
  • Unclear Requirements
  • Protects Employees from Unfair Blame: If a project stalls because a client hasn't sent credentials, the task is marked as an external blocker so the team member's delivery score isn't penalized.

Cycle Time & Quality Rework Tracking

  • Delivery Speed: Measures how many hours or days it takes from starting a task to getting it approved.
  • Rework Counter: Automatically tracks whenever a task fails review and gets sent back to In Progress, helping teams spot confusing requirements early.

Company Task Tracker Grid

  • Sort & Filter Everything: View tasks across projects by owner, priority (Low, Medium, High, Critical), status, and due date.
  • Activity Audit History: Every status shift, priority update, and comment is logged with the user's name and exact time.

5. Local Zero-Retention Voice-to-Task AI

Speak Naturally in 11 Indian Languages + English

  • Multilingual Input: Team members in the field or in the office can speak in English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, or Urdu.

Turn Speech into Tasks in Seconds

  • Understands Intent:
  • Say "Need to create the quarterly presentation by tomorrow afternoon" → Creates a new task in To-Do with tomorrow's due date.
  • Say "Spent three hours fixing the invoice calculation bug" → Moves the task to In Review and logs 3 hours of actual work.
  • Understands Everyday Hinglish: Intelligently handles mixed language commands and splits compound instructions (e.g. assigning one part to Priya and another to Rahul).

100% Private — Audio Deleted Immediately

  • Zero Cloud Exposure: Audio processing runs locally on your host server. The voice recording is processed in memory and shredded instantly. Nothing is ever sent to third-party cloud APIs or saved on disk.
  • Editable Preview Card: Before anything is saved, an editable confirmation card pops up so the user can verify the title, assignee, and hours with a single click.

Job Requisition Library

7. AI Assessment Maker & Secure Applicant Testing

Instant AI Test Generation from Any Job Description (JD)

  • Generate Tests in Seconds with AI: Super Admins and hiring managers can enter a role title or paste an entire Job Description, and MobiusEMS AI instantly generates a complete, tailored assessment with multiple-choice questions, detailed answer keys, and technical explanations.
  • Granular Exam Controls: Customize difficulty (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert), question count, strict time limits (e.g., 30 or 60 minutes), and custom passing percentages (e.g., 70%).
  • Custom Question Builder: Add custom domain scenarios, coding questions, or company-specific situational prompts alongside AI-generated questions.

Secure Applicant Portal with Auto-Generated Credentials

  • Dedicated Testing Credentials for Candidates: When evaluating new applicants, the system provisions an external candidate profile with dedicated, temporary login credentials (email & password).
  • Restricted Applicant Environment: When an applicant logs in, they are locked exclusively into the secure Assessment environment and cannot view any internal company data.
  • Timed Test Runner with Live Auto-Grading: Candidates take the test against a live countdown timer. Upon submission, the engine instantly grades their answers, calculates their final percentage score, and issues a detailed scorecard (Passed vs. Needs Improvement).

Internal Employee Upskilling & Benchmark Testing

  • Targeted Employee Testing: Assign specific assessments to individual employees or entire departments to benchmark technical proficiency, evaluate training outcomes, or qualify staff for promotions.

8. Onboarding Skill Justification & AI Evidence Ranking

Mandatory Onboarding Domain Skill Assessment

  • Role-Specific Skill Catalog: When an employee is onboarded, their assigned designation triggers a mandatory, domain-specific skill catalog configured by the Super Admin.
  • 1–10 Skill Rating with Mandatory Written Justification: For every skill in their domain, the employee must rate their proficiency from 1 to 10 AND provide a detailed, written implementation justification note (explaining Situation, Action, Result, and practical evidence).
  • Permanent Submission Lock: Once submitted during onboarding, the self-ratings and justification notes are permanently locked and cannot be edited.

Continuous AI Work-Evidence Monitor

  • Automated Output Tracking: As the employee works day-to-day, an internal AI monitor continuously compares their claimed 1–10 ratings against their actual completed tasks, on-time delivery rates, and manager quality review scores.
  • Real-Time Justification Status: Every skill rating is automatically categorized:
  • Justified: Tangible completed deliverables and high quality scores corroborate the claimed rating.
  • In Progress: Work is actively underway with positive velocity.
  • Needs Evidence: Daily deliverable output does not yet substantiate the self-claimed rating.

Super Admin Justification Leaderboard & Ranking

  • Transparency for Leadership: Super Admins and leadership have a dedicated dashboard ranking employees by their Evidence Confidence Score (%) and rating justification ratio (e.g., 8 of 10 skills justified).
  • Eliminates Resume & Rating Inflation: Ensures hiring and appraisal decisions are backed by measurable output, highlighting who truly delivers at the level they claimed.

Skills Inventory & Competency Catalog

10. Fair, Data-Backed Performance Reviews

Strategic Goals & Measurable KPIs

  • Cascading Goals: Connect high-level company milestones down to departments and individual contributors.
  • Clear Numbers, Not Vague Feedback: KPIs track actual numbers against target benchmarks, bounded between 0% and 200% achievement.

360-Degree Review Rubrics

  • Balanced Input: Evaluates feedback across managers, peers, and self-reviews using structured competency rubrics (execution, teamwork, communication, and ownership).

Transparent Mathematical Scoring

  • Clear Formula: Your team's performance rating is calculated objectively:
    Overall Rating = (Goal Achievement × Weight) + (KPI Results × Weight) + (360 Peer Reviews × Weight)
  • Objective Performance Tiers:
  • 90%+ : Exceptional Performer
  • 75% – 89.9% : Strong Performer
  • 60% – 74.9% : Consistent Performer
  • Below 60% : Developing / Support Needed

Immutable Review Records

  • Locked for Accuracy: Once an appraisal cycle closes, all scores, reviews, and weighting calculations are permanently locked. They cannot be edited after the fact, ensuring complete audit integrity.
